◆ CONCEPTUAL GLOSSARY
Game Space
Structural environment reconfigured by hegemonic power; the arena in which systemic rules operate and self-reproduce.
Spatial Fix
Geographical anchoring of excess capital to resolve over-accumulation crises; Harvey's spatial logic of capital expansion.
Consent
Gramscian legitimation of hegemony through cultural and institutional absorption of subordinate group interests and worldviews.
Downward Causation
Structural architecture dictating and locking-in entity behavior; emergent macro-level constraints feeding back onto constituent actors.
Systemic Forces
Interconnected economic, political-ideological, military, and technological variables driving power transitions within the hegemonic order.
Nodes
Institutions, individuals, or devices acting as centralized hubs controlling network flows of capital, data, norms, and coercive power.
