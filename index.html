THEORETICAL GLOSSARY

  • Game Space: The structural environment reconfigured by infrastructures and norms.
  • Systemic Forces: Pressures (tech/econ) shaping system evolution.
  • Consent: The moral/political legitimation that sustains material expansion.
  • Possibility Space: The menu of options available to subaltern entities.
  • Generative Entrenchment: Deep embedding of infrastructures creating lock-in.
  • Downward Causation: Structural architecture dictating entity behavior.
  • Relational Power: Power emerging from network position, not isolated capacity.
  • Systemic Disturbances: Frictions that accumulate toward a phase shift.
  • Entities: Nodes (infrastructure/capital) with causal powers.
