SYSTEMIC FORCES

Relational Power & Downward Causation

THEORETICAL GLOSSARY

  • Game Space: The structural environment reconfigured by the hegemon's infrastructures and norms.
  • Systemic Forces: Interlocking pressures (technological, economic) shaping system evolution.
  • Consent: The moral/political legitimation that sustains material expansion without overt coercion.
  • Possibility Space: The constrained or expanded "menu of options" available to actors.
  • Generative Entrenchment: Deep embedding of infrastructures creating irreversible systemic lock-in.
  • Downward Causation: The structural force by which the system's architecture dictates entity behavior.
  • Relational Power: Power emerging from an entity's position within networks, not isolated capacity.
  • Systemic Disturbances (Frictions): Organic crises that accumulate tension toward a phase shift.
  • Entities: States, institutions, infrastructures, and capital acting as nodes with causal powers.
